Omelette Recipe

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • Oil or butter to coat the pan
  • A pinch of salt and pepper
  • Any additional ingredients you wish to use as filling, such as bell peppers, spinach, ham, etc.

Steps

  1. Whisk eggs with salt and pepper in a bowl until fluffy
  2. Heat oil or butter in a large pan (8-inch non-stick pan or larger) at medium-low heat.
  3. Pour the egg mix into the pan as an even layer. Then, turn the heat to low.
  4. Let cook for 2-3 minutes without touching the mix.
  5. Add optional ingredients as omelette filling onto one side of the omelette.
  6. Gently fold the other half of the omelette over.
  7. Slowly slide the omelette onto a plate to be served.
